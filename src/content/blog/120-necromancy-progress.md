1. Boost Necromancy by consuming an Extreme Necromancy Potion.
2. Use three Attraction 3 glyphs.
3. Use one Speed 3 glyph in the Necromancy skillcape.
4. Wear the Alteration necklace.

It should be noted that although this process aids in generating a high amount of experience points per hour, constant attention is required due to the high attraction percentage. Most disturbances are quick to take care of but Defile is finicky at times.
