## Welcome to Gielinor

RuneScape is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) set in the fantasy world of Gielinor. Whether you're a returning veteran or a brand new adventurer, this guide will help you get started on your journey.

### What is RuneScape?

RuneScape offers a vast open world where you can:

- Train 28 different skills
- Complete hundreds of quests
- Battle fearsome monsters
- Trade with other players
- Build your own virtual life

### Getting Started

When you first log in, you'll find yourself in Tutorial Island (or the new player experience in RuneScape 3). Here are some tips to help you begin:

1. **Complete the tutorial** - It teaches you the basics of movement, combat, and skills
2. **Choose your path** - Decide if you want to be a warrior, mage, ranger, or a mix
3. **Start questing** - Quests are a great way to explore the world and earn rewards
4. **Join a clan** - Find a community to help you on your journey

### Early Goals

Here are some achievable goals for new players:

- Complete all free-to-play quests
- Get your combat stats to level 40
- Make your first 100,000 gold pieces
- Train a gathering skill like Mining or Woodcutting

### Final Thoughts

RuneScape is a game that rewards patience and creativity. There's no wrong way to play - whether you want to focus on combat, skills, questing, or just socializing with other players, the world of Gielinor has something for everyone.

Happy adventuring!
