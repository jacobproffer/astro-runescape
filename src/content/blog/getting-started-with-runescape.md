At long last, I've achieved level 120 Slayer on RuneScape. Since the release of the skill in 2005, Slayer was mostly an ignored skill until I went for max back in 2020. I suppose I've always been more of a skiller. Though I have made an effort to have the best gear and unlocks for Slayer throughout the years. I enjoyed unlocking the various Slayer helmets, and the slayer helmet stand on Anachronia. Though as much as I am happy to have the Slayer master cape now, I do have to admit that the bulk of the experience points for Slayer in the past year have came from the daily Treasure Hunter keys and events, such as last year's Christmas event. Anytime I received a lamp or star, I'd dump it into Slayer. In fact, I still have 25.6 million bonus experience for Slayer.

## Looking Forward

Although I've reached 120 Slayer, I've been interested in starting a Slayer tab in my bank. I don't currently have any interest in a push to 200 million experience in the skill but am interested in trying to make money from it. Anytime I watch someone's bank video, they seem to have one. My only current issue is that I need to make more space in my bank.
