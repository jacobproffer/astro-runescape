Over the past week, I've been chipping away at Necromancy. Typically, I'll do around 2-3 million experience most nights while watching a movie in the background. Last night, I reached level 116 Necromancy and achieved a combat level of 149. Although I still have a ways to go until 120, I am getting closer.

In a previous post, I had mentioned having issues with the Defile disturbance. The issue I found is that while siphoning, it is possible for a hissing sound to occur without purple smoke appearing. This means that if you're playing without sound, you can easily fail the disturbance siphon. But I suppose you could instead pay attention to the siphon progress bar.
