Over the past week, I've been chipping away at Necromancy. Typically, I'll do around 2-3 million experience most nights while watching a movie in the background. Last night, I achieved level 116 Necromancy, along with combat level of 149. I still have around four million bonus experience for Necromancy, which should last me until at least 117. Although I still have a ways to go until 120, I am getting closer to the goal.

In a previous post, I had mentioned having issues with the Defile disturbance. The issue I found is that while siphoning, it is possible for a hissing sound to occur without purple smoke appearing. This means that if you're playing without sound, you can easily fail the disturbance siphon. But you could instead pay attention to the siphon progress bar.
